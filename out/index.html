Minecraft Seed Finder

Pick a version, a biome and the structures you want near spawn. Seeds are searched in your browser and ranked by distance.

How it works

MC Masters uses Cubiomes compiled to WebAssembly — the same world-generation algorithms Minecraft uses — so maps match the game. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.
